# Acknowledgments and AI assistance

This project was developed by Piotr Łyczko as part of a master's research project
at Gdańsk University of Technology, supervised by Dr. habil. Marcin Ciecholewski.

The idea for PCam-D4Loc originated with the author and was developed through
brainstorming with ChatGPT. Codex assisted with writing, modifying, and checking
code, tests, analysis scripts, and documentation. ChatGPT and Codex also helped
with draft text, translation, literature organization, and the presentation and
interpretation of results. The author is responsible for the final project,
methodological decisions, and interpretation.

Numerical results come from model experiments and calculations on saved
predictions. They are not language-model estimates. The current release includes
corrected average-precision summaries that group equal scores at one threshold.

The project builds on the PCam and CAMELYON datasets, PyTorch and torchvision,
and the work cited in [the bibliography](references/references.bib). Reconstructed
methods are identified as such in [Methods](docs/METHODS.md); the implementation
should not be attributed to the original authors of those papers.
